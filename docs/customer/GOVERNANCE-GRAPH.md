# Governance Graph

The Governance Graph shows how this organization’s third-party records connect: vendors, assessments, evidence, findings, residual-risk snapshots, and decisions.

It is a relationship layer, not a separate product. Scores, files, and decisions still live on their original pages.

## What you can do

- Search objects in this organization
- Filter by node type, relationship type, status, and provenance
- Open an object’s direct relationships, impact, and lineage
- Move from a relationship to the authoritative record

## What it does not do

- It does not invent connections. If an assessment is not stored as the source of a finding, that link will not appear.
- It does not replace vendor, assessment, evidence, finding, or decision pages.
- It does not share another customer’s graph.
- It does not run AI-created relationships as facts. Suggested links stay suggested until a person approves them.

## Where to find it

Open **Governance Graph** under Insights, or use **Relationships** on a vendor, assessment, evidence, finding, or decision.
