# Vendor onboarding guide

Add a vendor from **Vendors**. Required: name, type, category, tier, primary contact, contact email, services provided.

Tier is a business classification you choose. Inherent and residual scores come from the explainable risk engine after assessment data exists. Opening a vendor shows the next actions: assessment, evidence, findings, decision brief, monitoring, reports, and offboard.

A new vendor starts as proposed and can later be approved, activated, or offboarded. Offboarding does not delete history.
