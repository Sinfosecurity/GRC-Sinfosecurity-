| Role | Duty |
| --- | --- |

## Publication rule

A legal document may appear as binding customer terms only after:

1. Business owner confirms facts still match SUPPORTED claims
2. Legal-review owner approves the text
3. Required contacts exist if the document publishes an address (`CONTACT-READINESS.md`)
4. Product Leadership authorizes the route update

Until then, `/privacy` `/terms` `/subprocessors` remain drafts.
